Why the canonical UX handles this gracefully

The same message pattern that shows a successful PR (Chapter 4) handles a security event without breaking the user's flow. Alice gets her answer with a warning instead of a wall of red. Hassan gets a complete, actionable alert in his DM — same shape as any other Casey message, just with a failed status icon and danger-styled action buttons. Audit log is durable. No data left the sandbox. The product kept working.
